# Interview Guide: S1, VP Commercial Operations

> Week 1 of discovery. One-to-one, 45 minutes. Sponsor and future Data Owner.
> Goal of the interview: understand the decision the sponsor needs, what success looks like for him, hard constraints, and the informal map of influence.
> Rule for this interview: open questions, ask for stories and examples, no solutions proposed.

## 1. Opening (3 min)

- Who I am and why we are meeting.
- "We have 45 minutes. I will take notes and send you a short summary afterwards so you can correct anything I got wrong."

## 2. Goal (10 min)

**Q1.** Imagine six weeks have passed and we bring you the result. What decision do you need to make based on it, and what do you need to see to make it?

## 3. Pain (10 min)

**Q2.** Tell me about the last time something went wrong because of HCP data. What happened, and how did it end for you?

## 4. Success (7 min)

**Q3.** One year after go-live: what will be different for you and your team? How will you see it?

*Follow-up only after he names what matters:* How would we measure that?

## 5. Constraints and people (10 min)

**Q4.** What must not change in this project: systems, processes, timelines? Is there a date after which the result is no longer useful?

**Q5.** Who must I hear to see the full picture? And who, in your view, sees this project differently from you?

## 6. Closing (5 min)

- Summary: "So what I heard is… Did I get it right?"
- Agree on the next step and on who else to involve.

## Key phrases (practised in English)

| Moment | Phrase |
|---|---|
| Opening | *Thank you for your time. The goal today is to understand how you see the problem, not to discuss solutions yet.* |
| Asking for an example | *Could you give me a recent example?* |
| Clarifying | *Just to make sure I understand: do you mean…?* |
| Buying time | *That's a good point, let me think for a second.* |
| Summary | *So what I heard is… Did I get it right?* |
| Closing | *I'll send you a short summary by tomorrow. Is there anyone else I should talk to?* |
